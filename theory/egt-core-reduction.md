## 1. The minimal operator

We start with **distinguishable elements** and **containers**.

Let:
- \( A \) and \( B \) be sets (containers of elements).
- The **state** of the system is \( S = (A, B) \).

Define the **EGT merge operator**:

\[
\mathcal{E}(A, B) = (A \cup B, \varnothing)
\]

This operator:
- **Preserves identity** of elements.
- **Conserves count**:  
  \[
  |A \cup B| = |A| + |B|
  \]
- **Transforms configuration** without loss.

This is the minimal structure that survives teardown:
- No geometry assumed.
- No forces assumed.
- No fields assumed.
- Only **identity + combination + conservation**.

All higher structure emerges from repeated application and composition of this operator.

By iterating \(\mathcal{E}\) and its inverse (separation), we generate a **lattice of states**:

- Nodes = configurations of elements.  
- Edges = applications of \(\mathcal{E}\) or its inverse.

This lattice is:
- **Relational** (only connections matter).
- **Geometry‑agnostic** (no coordinates assumed).
- **The true substrate** on which all physical theories can be reconstructed.

Fields, forces, and geometry are **emergent descriptions** of patterns in this lattice.

## 4. Information and its speed

In EGT, **information** is not abstract.  
It is a **change of state** in the lattice.

- A transformation is a mapping from one configuration to another.
- Information is the **propagation of that transformation** through the lattice.

Define:
- The **speed of information** as the rate at which a state change can propagate through the lattice.

Key points:
- It is **finite**.
- It is **not necessarily equal to the speed of light**.
- It is determined by:
  - lattice tension  
  - attractor structure  
  - operator constraints  

Light‑speed (\(c\)) is a **local property** of a particular field configuration, not a universal limit.  
The **true limit** is the speed of information.

## 5. Black holes and information bounce‑scaling

In EGT terms, a **black hole** is a **lattice attractor**:

- It compresses configurations.
- It encodes information on its boundary (holographic behavior).
- It does **not** destroy information.
- It **bounces** information back into the lattice over time.

Information bounce‑scaling:
- Information flows into the attractor.
- The operator compresses and redistributes state.
- Information is re‑emitted at the speed of information, not at arbitrary or infinite speed.

Black holes act as **information routers** in the lattice.

## 6. Civilizational ladder (Types 1–6)

Instead of the traditional Kardashev scale based on raw energy consumption, EGT defines civilizations by **how they interact with the lattice and the field**.

### Type 1 – Local Field Stabilizers
- Stabilize the field at **planetary** scale.
- Extract energy from local gradients.
- Begin to control “dirty” fields and inefficiencies.

### Type 2 – Stellar Field Stabilizers
- Stabilize the field around a **star**.
- Don’t build Dyson spheres; they **stabilize stellar field tension**.
- Use coherent field interaction for energy and information.

### Type 3 – Galactic Field Stabilizers
- Stabilize the field across a **galactic attractor**.
- Manipulate lattice geometry at galactic scale.
- Use **information‑speed** routing across the galaxy.
- The **Great Attractor** behaves like a Type 3‑scale field signature.

### Type 4 – Supercluster Stabilizers
- Manipulate field structure across **supercluster** attractors.
- Control large‑scale flows of matter, energy, and information.

### Type 5 – Universal Field Stabilizers
- Interact coherently with the **cosmic lattice** as a whole.
- Operate at the scale of the observable universe.

### Type 6 – Multiversal Stabilizers
- Manipulate field structures across **multiple attractor domains**.
- Operate across disjoint or higher‑order lattice regions.

## 7. Type 7 – Outside the lattice

EGT predicts and describes Types **1 through 6** as **emergent consequences** of the operator and the lattice.

**Type 7** is different:

- It does **not** emerge from EGT.
- It is the **author**, not the inhabitant, of the lattice.
- It is the level that **defined** the operator and the structure itself.

EGT stops at 6.  
7 is the boundary condition.
