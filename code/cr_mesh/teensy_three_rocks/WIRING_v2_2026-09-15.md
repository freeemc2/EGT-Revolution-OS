## Pin assignment (Teensy 4.1)

| coil (label the former) | drive pin | series R | runner sense pin | firmware index |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **C1** | **pin 2** | 220 R | **A0 = pin 14** | k = 0 |
| **C2** | **pin 3** | 220 R | **A1 = pin 15** | k = 1 |
| **C3** | **pin 4** | 220 R | **A2 = pin 16** | k = 2 |

Why these: pins 2/3/4 are plain GPIO, adjacent, no conflicting bus. **Pin 1 (old C3 drive) is Serial1 TX — dropped.**
Pins 14/15/16 (A0/A1/A2) are readable by BOTH ADC engines on the 4.1, so any pair can be sampled simultaneously.
Reserved, do not use: 0/1 (Serial1), 18/19 (I2C SDA/SCL — for the RM3100 magnetometer later), 11/12/13 (SPI + LED).

## Per-coil connections (identical for all three)

- winding **north** end -> 220 R -> drive pin. Keep the 220 R at the Teensy end, short lead.
- winding **south** end -> GND rail.
- runner **south** end -> A-pin. runner **north** end -> GND rail.
- **100 R shunt** from each A-pin to GND at the Teensy (already installed 2026-09-15 — verify they are on 14/15/16).
- Every GND lands on ONE star point on the Teensy GND pin. No daisy-chained grounds between coils.
- Twist each drive pair (drive-pin lead + its GND return) and each runner pair (A-pin lead + its GND return).
  Route drive pairs and runner pairs on opposite sides of the rig.

## Meter checklist before power (Brian)

1. Each winding end-to-end: ~1 ohm (22 AWG, one strand). Each runner end-to-end: < 1 ohm.
2. Winding to runner, same coil: OPEN. Winding to former/other coil: OPEN.
3. Each 220 R: 220 +/- 5%. Each 100 R shunt: 100 +/- 5%, from A-pin to GND.
4. With the Teensy unpowered: drive pin to GND reads ~221 ohm (220 R + winding). A-pin to GND reads ~100 ohm (shunt || runner).
5. Label C1/C2/C3 on the formers and on the wires. The label is the truth; the script names follow the label.
