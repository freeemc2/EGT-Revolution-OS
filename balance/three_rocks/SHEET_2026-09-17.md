## 1. The rig (Brian's numbers)

- Quad (n=4 star-quad twisted bundle), x3 identical: 3in sch40 PVC, OD 3.500 in; **64 turns** of a **4-strand 22 AWG** bundle, 65 ft per strand, twist lay 11.0 in (one rotation per turn); winding 8.75 in (L/D = 2.5); runner **18 AWG** straight axial under the windings.
- Drive: square 0..3.3 V (GPIO), fundamental amplitude (4/pi)*1.65 V = 2.101 V, 220 R in series, pin -> 220R -> winding north; winding south -> GND bus. Sense: I=mean(s*cos), Q=mean(s*sin), mag=sqrt(I^2+Q^2) -> mag = A/2 counts for a sine of amplitude A counts; phase=atan2(Q,I): in-phase-with-drive reads 90 deg by construction.
- Hand variance: 2-3 in of lead between the three. EM: dR = 0.256-0.385 %, dL ~ 0, drive-current change at 12 Hz 0.00183 % — unmeasurable in band: the 220R sets the current, the coil's own R is 0.1-0.5% of it, leads do not change L/D. EGT: nil — 'the coils don't care': L/D unchanged, r unchanged; a 2-3 in lead does not move the geometry.
- **Unknowns the sheet still needs (Brian/Tonic):** which strands are on the drive pin (1 / 4 parallel / 4 series); today's center-to-center spacing and axis angles; the shared ground resistance R_sh (measure it: 4-wire across the drive-return path).

**What each EM path can and cannot do:** inductive is proportional to f and reads in quadrature (0/180): at 12 Hz its ceiling is 3 orders of magnitude under the readings. Capacitive is proportional to f and only matters above ~10 kHz.
